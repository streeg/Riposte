[heading]Description[/heading]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/streeg/Riposte/main/media/circle-perfect.jpg[/img]

[b]Riposte shows you exactly when to dodge, parry, jump or gradient-parry, and forgives near misses.[/b]

A ring closes in on a dot and touches it the moment an enemy hit lands. The discs behind it are your character's real defense windows, drawn at the same scale, so the rule is simple: [b]press while the ring is inside the disc of the move you want.[/b]

In vanilla a defense either cancels a hit or does nothing. Riposte adds a [b]grace period[/b] around the game's own window: press a little too early or too late and you take reduced damage instead of the full hit.

Good for learning a tough boss, practicing parries, or just having an easier time.

[b]Known issues[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Most attacks need to be seen once.[/b] The first time an attack plays, Riposte learns its timing and shows "NEW ATTACK · LEARNING TIMING" instead of a ring. From then on the ring is shown in every later fight and session.[/*]
[*]The first time a jump attack is seen it may be labelled as a normal attack.[/*]
[*]Some melee attacks where the enemy runs to you can land at a slightly different time on each run.[/*]
[*]The late refund uses the game's normal "set HP", so Pictos that block healing can interfere with it.[/*]
[/list]

[heading]Main features[/heading]
[b]Timing circle[/b]
[list]
[*]One ring per upcoming hit, reaching the centre exactly at impact[/*]
[*]Green = parry window, blue = dodge window, yellow = grace window[/*]
[*]Orange = jump attack, purple = gradient attack; the label under the circle names the move to use[/*]
[*]Windows are drawn for the character actually being attacked, so the circle follows window-size mods like Easier or Harder Dodge and Parry[/*]
[/list]

[b]Grace period[/b]
[list]
[*]Up to 0.25 s too early: the hit is reduced before it lands[/*]
[*]Up to 0.20 s too late: part of the damage is given back as HP[/*]
[*]90 % reduction at the edge of the game's window, fading to 0 % at the far edge[/*]
[*]Near misses never trigger counters; buff and Lumina damage are never touched[/*]
[/list]

[b]Learning[/b]
[list]
[*]Attacks the game doesn't announce are learned the first time they play; after that the ring is accurate to about 15 ms and saved for every later session[/*]
[*][b]Ctrl+F8[/b] re-learns a single attack if its ring looks wrong[/*]
[/list]

[b]Settings and presets[/b]
[list]
[*]Everything is in [i]riposte_config.lua[/i]: window sizes, reduction, colours, position, and a latency offset for TVs and wireless controllers[/*]
[*]Optional presets: [b]Forgiving[/b], [b]Strict[/b], [b]Trainer Only[/b] (circle only, vanilla damage), [b]Grace Only[/b] (no circle)[/*]
[/list]

[b]Performance[/b]
[list]
[*]Does nothing outside battle and never writes to disk mid-fight[/*]
[/list]
